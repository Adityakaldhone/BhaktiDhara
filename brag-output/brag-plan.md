# Brag Plan: BhaktiDhara (भक्तिधारा)

## What is this app?
A devotional app that turns your phone into a daily temple: aartis with synced, auto-scrolling lyrics, a real brass ghanti and shankh you can sound, 25+ deities, Panchang, granths, and it all works offline.

## The angle
"The temple rings first." Most app videos open on a phone. This one opens on a sound: the app's own recorded brass ghanti. The video treats the product with the reverence of a morning puja (calm, warm, unhurried) and lets the real rituals inside the app (bell, conch, flower shower, lyrics gliding line by line) do the bragging. Nothing is invented: every word on screen is the project's own copy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A golden temple bell (the app's own `bell.png`) swings into frame on warm cream and strikes; the real `ghanti.mp3` from the app rings on the strike. As the bell settles, the line arrives word by word: **"Your Sacred Daily Temple"**. The sound earns the next 18 seconds before any UI appears.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero aarti card from the site, live: **Shree Hanuman Chalisa · LIVE AARTI**, Devanagari lyric lines highlighting and gliding upward one by one, the way the app auto-scrolls them.
- A tap on **Ring Ghanti** (bell sound), then **Blow Shankh** (the app's real conch), then **Shower Flowers**: marigold petals fall across the card and carry the viewer into the next scene.
- **25+ Sacred Deities**: real deity artwork from the app (Ganesh, Hanuman, Shiva, Krishna, Rama, Lakshmi, Durga, Vitthal) arriving one by one on the beat, then three feature labels: Daily Vedic Panchang, Holy Granth & Pothi Reader, 100% Offline Ready.

## Outro / punchline
The ground floods from cream to deep maroon. The app icon (संपूर्ण आरती, diya and Om) lands on a strong beat, followed by **BhaktiDhara · भक्तिधारा** and the site's closing line **"Bring the Sacred Temple into Your Home"**, then a "Get it on Google Play" badge. Hold, then fade.

## User flow worth showing
Open an aarti (Shree Hanuman Chalisa) → lyrics auto-scroll in sync while you sing → tap Ring Ghanti / Blow Shankh / Shower Flowers during the puja. This is the centrepiece scene (Scene 2), recreated from the site's hero card, which mirrors the app's aarti screen.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet sunrise puja film: warm brass, marigold and maroon, reverent and unhurried
- Interpretation: four scenes with long, settled holds; soft transitions caused by objects on screen (petals, a colour flood); no jokes, no hype words, no fast cuts; the sound of the real ritual does the emotional work.

- Role: warm bed plus the app's own ritual sounds as the only real effects

- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12…music-cues.md`, ~110 BPM). Strong cues to target: **17.47 s** (app icon lands), **18.56 s** (Google Play badge). Beat-grid window for the deity sequence: half-beats from 11.46 s for the eight images; the three feature labels on 13.64, 14.20 and 14.73 s, then held together.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS makes the warm glow behind the bell and behind the app icon breathe slightly. No waveforms, no pulsing text.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: bell strike in the hook (real `ghanti.mp3`); tap on Ring Ghanti (real `ghanti.mp3`); tap on Blow Shankh (real `shankh_dhun.mp3`, trimmed); first and last deity arrival (soft drop); app icon landing (soft warm impact)
- Restraint rule: no whooshes on every cut, no synthesised chimes, nothing louder than the real bell

"Your Sacred Daily Temple" arrives word by word (Mukta 700, large, maroon/ink with "Temple" in saffron-dark), then a quieter line: "Aarti Sangrah & Holy Granths". Hold until the transition.

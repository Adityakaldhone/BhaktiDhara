BhaktiDhara ("we," "our," or "us") developed the BhaktiDhara: Aarti Sangrah app (the "App") as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy informs you about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your privacy. Our App does not collect any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not require user registration or login.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any PII.
  • We do not store any user-generated content.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information independently:

1. Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General usage and interaction data

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

2. YouTube (Embedded Videos)

The App embeds YouTube videos using a secure in-app WebView. When you play a video, YouTube's servers may collect:

  • IP address
  • Video viewing data
  • Device information

Videos are loaded via YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode where possible. For more information, see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Google Fonts

The App uses Google Fonts for typography. Google may log font requests with associated IP addresses. See the Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.

Device Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

Permission Purpose
Internet Access Required to load YouTube videos, advertisements, and fonts
Wake Lock Keeps the screen on during aarti reading/pooja so you can pray without interruption
We do not request access to your camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location.

Data Storage

  • All aarti content (lyrics, catalog data) is bundled within the App and stored locally on your device.
  • Your language preference is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server.
  • We do not maintain any external database or server that stores your personal data.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not require registration and does not collect PII, no child-specific data is gathered.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through a third-party service within the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
